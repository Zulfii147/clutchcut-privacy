ClutchCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Contact: zulfiqarh61@gmail.com

The short version

ClutchCut does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access: your clips are analysed and edited entirely on your phone and never leave it.

What stays on your device

  • The clips you pick, the copies ClutchCut makes while you edit, and your finished Shorts.
  • Your settings (for example, the game type you chose and your edit choices).

Deleting a project or uninstalling the app removes ClutchCut's copies. Shorts you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them.

Permissions

  • Photo Picker: you choose one clip at a time. ClutchCut can't see the rest of your gallery.
  • Notifications (optional): to show export progress.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): to save your Short to the gallery.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share menu sends your video to the app you choose (for example YouTube or TikTok). What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy.

Ratings

After you share, ClutchCut may ask Google Play to show its standard "rate this app" prompt. This is handled by Google Play; ClutchCut doesn't receive your rating or any personal data.

Children

ClutchCut is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
